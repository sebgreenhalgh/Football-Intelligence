How to review
Step through the one viewer or play the interval. Observed detections are solid cyan; predictions are dashed amber and off by default. Answer the four questions, confirm the suggested conclusion, add one concise note, then save.
Keyboard: Left/Right step frames, Space play/pause, Shift+Left/Shift+Right jump phases, Escape close help.
