          REVIEW TASK
          
You are not identifying people, assigning slots, correcting every detector box, interpreting track IDs, or judging whether a spatial rectangle follows a person.

        

        

          

            1 Before the interval, are at least two distinct people independently visible?
            

            
Look for two separately visible people across more than one frame.

          

          

            2 What happens during the interval?
            

          

          

            3 After the interval, are at least two distinct people independently visible again?
            

          

          

            4 Is it visually plausible that the incoming paths continue into the outgoing paths?
            

            
Exact incoming-to-outgoing identity mapping is not required.

How to review
Step through the one viewer or play the interval. Observed detections are solid cyan; predictions are dashed amber and off by default. Answer the four questions, confirm the suggested conclusion, add one concise note, then save.
Keyboard: Left/Right step frames, Space play/pause, Shift+Left/Shift+Right jump phases, Escape close help.
